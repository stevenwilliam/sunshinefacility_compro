# 14 — User guide (CMS)

For the people who edit the website and read the inbox. Sign in at
**sunshinefacility.sunshinefood.co.id/admin** (www.sunshinefacility.com/admin once that domain is live). Forgotten your password? Ask a
superadmin to set a new one (Users → your name → Reset password).

## Your role decides what you see

| You are | You can |
|---|---|
| **Editor** | change every text and image on the site; upload images; edit contact details, locations, job types, building types, brand colours |
| **Sales** | read quotation requests |
| **HR** | read job applications and open CVs |
| **Superadmin** | everything, plus users, notification recipients and other settings, audit history, outgoing mail |

## Every list works the same way

- **Search** box: type any words; the list narrows as you type.
- **Filters** beside it where useful (page, service line, list, group…).
- **Export CSV** downloads exactly what you see, separated by `|` (pipe) so Indonesian addresses with commas open correctly in Excel. It is greyed out — with the reason under it — when nothing matches.
- **Add** is greyed out where rows are fixed by the site (for example Pages and Site text), and says why.
- The **pencil** opens the editor on the right; the **bin** deletes after a confirmation. A greyed-out bin has a tooltip saying why.

## The editor

- Fields marked * are required. **Save** stays grey with "To save, fill in: …" until they are filled.
- Every text has an English and an Indonesian box. Fill both.
- Images: **Choose from media** opens the library with a search box.
- **Published** off = hidden from the site. **Placeholder** on = shown with a "Placeholder" tag (see below).
- Changes are live on the site as soon as you save.

## Where each thing on the site is edited

| On the site | In the CMS |
|---|---|
| Big headings and their paragraph on any page | **Pages** (filter by page) |
| Buttons, labels, small text, form messages, footer lines, SEO titles | **Site text** (search for the words you see) |
| The five service lines: names, descriptions, colours, photo | **Services** |
| Tags under each service, figures (5M+ meals…), "What's included" | **Service tags & points**, **Key figures**, **What's included** |
| Sectors on the Industries page and which services they use | **Industries** |
| Client logos | **Clients** |
| Certificates | **Certificates** |
| Job openings | **Careers** |
| Client quotes | **Testimonials** |
| Management team | **Management** |
| Why Sunshine points, journey timeline, mission, values, Lippo sectors, "one contract…", proof points, reasons to join | **Lists** (filter by list) |
| 10+ years, 18+ cities… | **Stats** (Home and About separately) |
| Phone, WhatsApp, email, address, hours, map pin, social links | **Parameters → Contact details / Social links** |
| Building types in the form, locations, job types | **Parameters** (option groups — you can add rows) |

## Placeholders

Some content is provisional (contact details, social links, testimonials,
management, openings, vision and mission). It shows on the site with a
dashed **Placeholder** tag, and the sidebar counts how many remain.
**Change the content and the tag disappears by itself.** To confirm content
without changing it, untick *Placeholder*.

## Certificates

- A certificate past its *Valid until* date shows **Expired** and cannot be published: the switch is greyed out and says why. Upload the renewed certificate in Media, open the certificate, choose the new image, set the new date, publish.
- You get an email some weeks before a certificate expires. On the day after expiry it leaves the website by itself.

## Media library

- **Upload as** photo, logo, certificate or brand, then **Upload**. PNG, JPEG or WebP. The site makes sharp, small versions automatically.
- Click the pencil to set the name and the **alt text** in both languages (what a blind visitor's screen reader says).
- An image used somewhere cannot be deleted; the bin's tooltip says where to replace it first.

## Inbox (Sales, HR)

- **Quotation requests** / **Job applications**: newest first; click a row to see everything.
- The inbox is read-only. Reply to the client by phone, WhatsApp or email.
- HR: **Open CV** gives a link that works for five minutes; opening it is recorded.
- Applications are deleted automatically after the retention period (12 months unless changed).
